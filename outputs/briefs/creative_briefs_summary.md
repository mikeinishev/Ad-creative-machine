## 📐 Format Specs

### Format 1: Instagram Feed (1080x1080)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (Square)
- **Optimal Use**: Detailed value props, charts, income visuals
- **Attention**: 2-3 seconds

### Format 2: Instagram Stories/Reels (1080x1920)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (Vertical)
- **Optimal Use**: Quick hooks, urgency, mobile-first consumption
- **Attention**: 1-2 seconds
