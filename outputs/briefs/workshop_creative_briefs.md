Simple, stupid-simple arithmetic that proves the business model works — scribbled on a napkin.

## CREATIVE W7-A: "Coffee Shop Napkin"
The revenue calculation hits you while drinking coffee. Real marker on real napkin.

**Format:** Static image, 1080×1080 + 1080×1920

### Visual Concept

A white cocktail napkin (the thick, square, slightly textured bar napkin type) lying flat on a dark walnut wood coffee table. The napkin is slightly rotated 5-7° clockwise from straight — it looks tossed down casually, not staged.

On the napkin, handwritten in BLACK SHARPIE marker (thick, slightly uneven strokes — real handwriting, NOT a font):

```
10 clients × $300/mo
= $3,000/mo recurring
= $36,000/year ✓

No cold calling
No ad spend
AI does the work
```

The "$3,000/mo" line should be CIRCLED with the marker — a quick, confident oval drawn around it. A small arrow points from "AI does the work" down toward the bottom of the napkin. The handwriting should look like an entrepreneur scribbling excitedly during a coffee meeting — confident, slightly tilted lines, not perfectly horizontal.

The Sharpie ink should show realistic bleed into the napkin fibers — slightly feathered edges on thick strokes, darker where the marker paused.

### Surrounding Elements

1. **Black Sharpie marker** — cap off, lying diagonally below-right of the napkin. The cap is 3-4cm away from the pen. The felt tip shows slight ink staining.
2. **White ceramic coffee cup** — upper-right corner of frame. Half-full latte with visible crema surface (no latte art, just golden-brown crema). The cup casts a soft shadow toward bottom-left.
3. **iPhone 15 Pro (titanium)** — bottom-left edge of frame, screen face-down, slightly cut off by the frame edge. Just enough visible to read "iPhone" silhouette.
4. **Single coffee bean** — on the table surface between the napkin and the cup. A small authenticity detail.
5. **Faint water ring stain** — on the walnut table, partially under the napkin's upper-left corner. Old, dried — this table has been used.

### Mood & Lighting

Warm overhead café lighting, approximately 3500K color temperature. The napkin is THE BRIGHTEST element in frame — it almost glows against the dark wood. Soft, diffused shadows (overcast window light mixed with warm interior lamps). The overall mood is "this idea just hit me over coffee and I had to write it down." Slight warmth in the wood tones, cool blue-ish tones in the phone and shadows. Clean and crisp photography — no film grain, no filters. Think high-end Instagram flat-lay but NOT overly styled — it should feel spontaneous, not art-directed.
