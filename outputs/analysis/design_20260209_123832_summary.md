## Executive Summary

The Boomerang Product quiz funnel is a **6-screen lead generation and qualification** funnel designed for restaurant owners. It follows a classic B2B SaaS quiz pattern: welcome → qualification questions → personalized results → product offer.

### Funnel Flow
```
Welcome → Q1 (Restaurant Type) → Q2 (Pain Point) → Q3 (Volume) → Result → Offer
```

## Key Insights

### Funnel Characteristics
- **Target Audience**: Restaurant owners/managers (SMB)
- **Industry**: Restaurant / Hospitality
- **Product**: SaaS Customer Loyalty Platform
- **Conversion Goal**: Free trial signup or demo booking
- **Questions**: 3 (optimal for completion rate)
- **Personalization**: Results screen uses quiz data

### Value Proposition
**Primary Promise**: Increase repeat customers through digital loyalty  
**Mechanism**: Automated SMS loyalty program  
**Proof**: "10,000+ restaurants", "+25% repeat visits"

### Pain Points Addressed
1. Customer retention
2. Competition from delivery apps
3. Building loyalty programs
4. Tracking customer behavior

### Strengths
✅ Clear value prop from the start  
✅ Short quiz (3 questions = high completion)  
✅ Progress indicators reduce drop-off  
✅ Personalized results increase relevance  
✅ Strong social proof (10,000+ users)  
✅ Risk reversal (30-day guarantee)
