## 📋 Executive Summary

This is a **21-screen partner recruitment quiz funnel** designed to qualify and onboard implementation partners for Boomerang SaaS. The funnel uses progressive profiling, social proof, and localized lead previews to convert prospects into booked onboarding calls.

**Key Metrics**:
- 🎯 **21 total screens** (welcome → questions → qualification → lead capture → booking)
- 📧 **3 lead capture points** (ZIP code, email, calendar booking)
- ❓ **9 question screens** for segmentation and qualification
- ✅ **2 result/confirmation screens** providing positive reinforcement
- 💼 **5 offer screens** showcasing value proposition

## 🎯 Conversion Elements

### Trust Indicators
- **CNBC logo** (Screen 3) - Third-party credibility
- **"1,800+ partners"** - Social proof throughout
- **Partner testimonials** - Real quotes with names
- **US map visualization** - Geographic reach
- **Privacy badges** - Data protection assurances

### Urgency Triggers
- **"192 leads available"** - Scarcity
- **"Including 3 warm"** - Immediate opportunity
- **Timeline expectations** - "2 business days", "1st contract by Jul 18"
- **Specific lead addresses** - Real, actionable leads

### Value Proposition
- **$400-$1,000 MRR** potential
- **4-8 new clients per month** projection
- **Localized lead preview** with real business names
- **Low time commitment** (2-4 hours/week)

## 📊 Funnel Insights

### Strengths
1. **Progressive profiling** - Collects data gradually without overwhelming
2. **Personalization** - Dynamic content based on role and location
3. **Social proof layering** - Multiple trust signals throughout
4. **Concrete lead preview** - Shows actual businesses, not just numbers
5. **Clear value visualization** - Charts and timelines make outcomes tangible

### Lead Capture Strategy
- **3-step capture**: ZIP → Email → Calendar booking
- **Each step justified**: ZIP unlocks lead preview, email gets notifications, booking secures spot
- **Low friction**: Simple inputs, clear value exchange

### Screen Types Distribution
- **Questions**: 9 screens (43%)
- **Offers/Value**: 5 screens (24%)
- **Lead Capture**: 3 screens (14%)
- **Confirmations**: 2 screens (10%)
- **Education**: 2 screens (10%)
