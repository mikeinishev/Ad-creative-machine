## Skills examined

Local `ad-creative` (hook-system and short-form-video-specs), `video`, and
`seedance-higgsfield-loops` supplied workflow ideas. The last is for website loops;
its no-speech/minimal-motion aesthetic must not leak into UGC ads.

Public [Pika UGC skill](https://github.com/Pika-Labs/Pika-Plugins/blob/main/skills/ugc-ads/SKILL.md)
and [UGC video skill](https://github.com/Jarad-z/ugc-video-skill/blob/main/SKILL.md)
were read as reference material, not installed. Useful patterns: consistent creator
and product references, a reason to film, scene-specific action, reliable UI overlays.
Their vendor workflows and fixed templates were not adopted.

## Applied production decisions

- Three distinct hook variants per concept. Spoken hook and opening action establish
  relevance together. Short headlines add context; subtitles accurately repeat speech.
- About 30 seconds and six scenes for the first pack. Two 15-second clips are the current batch default; the existing pilot uses three
  10-second clips. Shot timing follows dialogue length.
- Workflow before/after, not invented testimonial outcomes. Keep generated-actor provenance internal. User explicitly forbids on-screen
  recreation/demo labels (2026-10-25). No historical ad sales cited as product results.
- Natural phone footage with ordinary activity. Remove fake growth graphs from old
  100plus reference plates before using them as scene references.
- Product UI inserts must be genuine captures with source/date and no personal data.
  If no appropriate capture exists, show the physical action and an unreadable screen;
  do not pretend this proves an exact interface.
- Simple captions, restrained cuts, ambient sound. No music (user 2026-10-25). Do not force a cut every second.
  Keep key information toward frame center and verify platform preview at export.
- Recruitment is one restaurant, never five. 100plus is a proposed target subject to
  current offer terms; a crowded-room montage cannot be evidence it was achieved.
- Editorial score and native-language QA are separate from paid conversion evidence.
  Only an actual controlled acquisition test establishes the better-performing ad.
