# Creative Brief Template (Opus-Style)

This template defines the exact format Agent 4 must follow when writing creative briefs for Agent 5 (Designer). Each brief is a standalone `.md` file.

---

## Template

```markdown
# CREATIVE #[N]: "[Creative Name]"
[One-line concept tagline — what the visual metaphor is]

**Format:** Static image, 1080×1080 (Instagram/Facebook feed) + 1080×1920 (Stories/Reels cover)

## Visual Concept

[PRIMARY SCENE — 20-40 lines of detailed description]

Describe the scene as if directing a photographer:
- What is the hero object/subject?
- What material is it made of? (wood type, metal finish, paper texture)
- What color, size, condition? (new, worn, slightly scratched)
- Where exactly is it in the frame? (center-left, upper-right, covering 60% of frame)
- What angle are we viewing from?

If screens/devices are shown:
- Describe EXACTLY what is on the screen
- Write out any text, UI elements, dashboard layouts, notification content
- Include specific numbers, names, labels

If people are shown:
- Age range, gender, ethnicity (or note "diverse for A/B testing")
- Clothing (specific: "casual button-down shirt", not "nice clothes")
- Posture and body language
- Expression (if face is visible)
- What their hands are doing
- Camera perspective (behind, side profile, over-shoulder — avoid direct face when possible)

If text/writing is part of the visual concept (receipt, napkin, sign):
- Write out the FULL text content with exact formatting
- Specify the "font" feel (monospaced receipt font, handwritten Sharpie, painted on wood)

## Surrounding Elements

[3-5 specific secondary objects that add authenticity]

For each object, specify:
- What it is (exact type: "black ballpoint pen", not "a pen")
- Where it is in the frame
- Its state (cap off, half-full, face-down)
- Blur level (sharp, slightly blurred, soft-focus)
- Purpose (texture, framing, authenticity, context)

Include 1-2 "authenticity details" — small imperfections that make the scene real:
- Water ring stain on wood
- A single coffee bean on the counter
- Slightly wrinkled napkin corner
- Dust particles visible in a sunbeam

## Mood & Lighting

[5-10 lines defining the emotional and physical light environment]

Include:
- Primary light source and direction ("natural daylight from upper left")
- Color temperature in Kelvin if relevant ("warm 3200K restaurant ambiance")
- Secondary light sources ("screen glow provides cool secondary light")
- Shadow quality ("soft shadows", "dramatic pool of focused light")
- Film/photo feel ("slight film grain", "clean and crisp", "matte finish")
- Emotional mood keywords ("aspirational but not flashy", "spontaneous coffee-shop energy")
- What is bright vs. dark in the frame

## Typography Overlay (to be added in post)

> These text elements are NOT part of the AI-generated image.
> They will be composited in post-production by Agent 5.

**Headline:** "[Hook text — the scroll-stopping line]"
**Subheadline:** "[Supporting copy + CTA]"
**Placement:** [Where on the final image — "bottom bar with semi-transparent dark background", "above the phone", etc.]

## Key Details for AI Model

[6-10 bullet points — critical constraints for image generation]

Always include:
- Style: Photorealistic rendering / Editorial illustration / Infographic with real textures
- Camera angle: Exact degrees or description (30-degree overhead, eye level, flat lay 90°)
- Depth of field: What's sharp vs. blurred
- Focal point: What the eye should go to FIRST
- Resolution: minimum 2048×2048 for cropping flexibility
- Legibility: Which text/content in the image MUST be readable
- What to AVOID: brand logos, specific trademarks, etc.
- Color temperature if critical
- Any "feel" constraints: "not too perfect", "should feel spontaneous", etc.
```

## Example: "The Napkin Math"

```markdown
# CREATIVE #4: "The Napkin Math"
Simple Revenue Calculation Scribbled on a Napkin

**Format:** Static image, 1080×1080 + 1080×1920

## Visual Concept

A white paper napkin (the thick, square cocktail napkin type you get at a bar 
or coffee shop) lying on a dark marble countertop or dark wooden coffee table. 
On the napkin, handwritten in black Sharpie marker (thick, slightly uneven 
strokes — real handwriting, not a font), the following calculation:

10 restaurants
× $300/mo
= $3,000/mo

You keep 70%
= $2,100/mo
       recurring 🔄

The handwriting should look confident but casual — like an entrepreneur quickly 
scribbling an idea. Some numbers might be underlined. The "$2,100/mo" at the 
bottom should be circled or boxed with the marker. Maybe a small arrow pointing to it.

## Surrounding Elements

- A black Sharpie marker lying next to the napkin (cap off, positioned diagonally)
- A coffee cup (white ceramic, half-full with a latte — visible from above, 
  showing latte art or at least creamy surface) positioned in the upper-right 
  corner of the frame
- The edge of an iPhone is visible at the bottom-left, screen off
- Perhaps a single coffee bean or two scattered on the surface for texture

## Mood & Lighting

Casual, authentic, "this idea just hit me at a coffee shop" energy. Overhead 
lighting, slightly warm. The napkin is the brightest element in the frame. 
Think Instagram flat-lay but not overly styled — it should feel spontaneous.

## Typography Overlay (to be added in post)

**Headline:** "The math is stupid simple."
**Subheadline:** "The hard part is knowing what to sell and how. Free 3-day workshop."
**Placement:** Above or below the napkin image.

## Key Details for AI Model

- The handwriting on the napkin is the HERO — it must be 100% legible and look 
  like real human handwriting with a thick marker
- Napkin texture: slightly textured paper, with subtle embossed pattern if possible
- The marker strokes should show slight bleed into the napkin (as real markers do)
- Not too perfect — this is spontaneous, not a designed infographic
- Camera angle: directly overhead (true flat lay, 90 degrees)
- Shallow depth of field is NOT needed here — everything in focus for flat lay
- Photorealistic rendering, NOT illustration
- Resolution: minimum 2048×2048
```
